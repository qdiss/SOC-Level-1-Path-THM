# Acceptable Use Policy - TryBike Parts (fictional company)

> Sample document created for learning purposes. TryBike Parts is a
> fictional online shop. Drafted with AI assistance and reviewed and
> edited by the author.

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Version | 1.0 |
| Owner | IT / Security Team |
| Review cycle | Every 12 months |
| Applies to | All employees, contractors, and temporary staff |

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how company systems, accounts, and data may be used
so that they stay secure, available, and compliant with legal
obligations.

## 2. Scope

The policy covers all company-owned or company-managed devices,
networks, cloud services, email, and any personal device used to access
company data.

## 3. Acceptable Use

- Use company systems for business purposes, with limited, reasonable personal use that does not affect work or security.
- Keep your own login credentials private and use a unique password and multi-factor authentication where available.
- Lock your screen when away from your device.
- Install only software approved by the IT team.

## 4. Prohibited Activities

- Sharing accounts or passwords, or using someone else's account.
- Attempting to bypass security controls, scan the network, or access systems without authorisation.
- Installing unlicensed or unapproved software.
- Storing company data on personal cloud storage or removable media without approval.
- Using company systems for illegal, harassing, or offensive activity.

## 5. Email and Phishing

- Treat unexpected links and attachments with suspicion and verify the sender before acting.
- Report suspected phishing emails to the Security Team instead of deleting or forwarding them.
- Never provide credentials in response to an email or phone request.

## 6. Data Handling

- Classify and handle data according to its sensitivity (public, internal, confidential).
- Customer personal data and payment data may only be accessed by authorised staff for business needs.
- Dispose of data and devices through approved procedures.

## 7. Use of AI Tools

- Only approved AI tools may be used for company work.
- Do not enter customer data, credentials, source code, or confidential information into public AI tools.
- AI-generated content must be reviewed by a person for accuracy before it is used or shared.

## 8. Monitoring

The company may monitor use of its systems for security and compliance
purposes, in line with applicable law and privacy requirements.

## 9. Incident Reporting

Report lost devices, suspected account compromise, malware, or any
suspicious activity to the Security Team immediately.

## 10. Enforcement

Violations may result in access restrictions, disciplinary action, or
legal action, depending on severity.
